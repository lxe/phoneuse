Free and open source · No jailbreak · No cable

Use your iPhone from your computer.

Your iPhone shows up in a window on your Linux PC or Mac. Click to tap, drag to swipe, scroll with your mouse wheel and type on your real keyboard.

  • Feels instant. The screen keeps up with your mouse.
  • Looks sharp. Your iPhone's real screen, in a window you can resize.
  • Your keyboard works. Type and use keyboard shortcuts.

Automate any workflow.

Let your agent use any app on your phone. No API needed.

  • Test your iOS app

    Run QA passes on a real device and report what broke.

  • Check sites on mobile

    Open pages in Safari and verify they look and work right.

  • Approve sign-ins

    Tap the 2FA prompt on your phone when your agent logs in.

  • Use apps without an API

    Book, order, reschedule and fill in forms in any app.

  • Catch up on notifications

    Read what came in and summarize what needs you.

  • Get data out of apps

    Copy readings, receipts and order history into your files.

  • Change settings

    Flip toggles and set up a phone the same way every time.

  • Capture screenshots

    Grab every screen of a flow for docs and App Store listings.

You're always in control.

Phone Use asks you before an agent can use your phone. While it works, a Live Activity shows each step, and you can take over at any time.
